读懂回合、资源与胜利条件Understand the rounds, resources, and victory conditions

以下内容根据当前已部署版本整理，用于帮助玩家快速掌握一局 HVT 的玩法结构。The summary below is based on the currently deployed version and is intended to help players understand how a full match works.

规则总览Rules Overview

地图与回合Map & Rounds

  • 地图包含 20 个节点，以及它们之间的连接关系。The battlefield contains 20 nodes connected as a network graph.
  • 一局最多 20 回合。A match lasts up to 20 rounds.
  • 攻击者先设定初始感染点，再进入正式回合流程。The attacker sets the initial infected node before the round loop begins.

胜利条件Victory Conditions

  • 攻击方胜利：active 节点数达到 7 个或以上。Attacker wins: 7 or more nodes become active.
  • 防守方胜利：所有感染节点被清除。Defender wins: all infected nodes are removed.
  • 时间胜利：若打满 20 回合仍未被攻陷，则防守方获胜。Time victory: if round 20 is reached without collapse, the defender wins.

节点状态Node States

状态State 含义Meaning 说明Notes
DD 普通干净节点Normal clean node 默认安全状态。Default safe state.
D 加固节点Hardened node 会降低传播成功率，并提高特殊攻击防御。Reduces spread success and strengthens defense against special attacks.
H 蜜罐节点Honeypot node 帮助防守方获得更多攻击线索；场上最多 3 个蜜罐。Helps the defender collect attack clues; at most 3 honeypots can exist.
HD 加固蜜罐Hardened honeypot 同时具备加固和蜜罐效果。Combines the effects of hardening and honeypot deployment.
lurk 潜伏感染Lurking infection 已被感染但尚未全面激活。The node is infected but not yet fully activated.
active 激活感染Active infection 攻击方的高威胁控制状态，也是主要胜利进度。High-threat attacker control state and the main progress toward attacker victory.
abandoned 废置节点Abandoned node 被摧毁后暂时退出网络，需要恢复后才能重新使用。Removed from normal play after destruction and must be restored before reuse.

回合流程Round Flow

1. 初始设置1. Setup

攻击方指定一个初始感染节点，并决定其初始状态为 lurk 或 active。The attacker chooses one initial infected node and sets it as either lurk or active.

2. 回合开始与资源结算2. Round Start & Resources

每回合开始时，攻击方获得人类能量与 AI 能量，防守方获得 budget。感染越多，攻击方获得的能量通常越多。At the start of each round, the attacker gains human energy and AI energy while the defender gains budget. Larger infections generally generate more attacker energy.

3. 攻击方 AI 阶段3. Attacker AI Phase

AI 自动执行常规传播与普通攻击，是攻击方持续施压的主要来源。The attacker AI automatically handles routine spreading and normal attacks, creating constant pressure.

4. 攻击方人类阶段4. Attacker Human Phase

玩家可执行激活、休眠、扫描、特殊攻击与 C2 迁移等关键动作。The human attacker performs key actions such as activation, sleep, scanning, special attacks, and C2 migration.

5. 防守方确认蜜罐5. Defender Honeypot Prompt

防守方可选择是否允许系统预测并预部署一个蜜罐。The defender may allow the system to predict and pre-deploy one honeypot.

6. 防守方人类阶段6. Defender Human Phase

玩家执行扫描、清除、夺回、恢复、流量清洗与反击 C2 等高价值响应动作。The defender performs high-value responses: scanning, clearing, recapturing, restoring, traffic cleansing, and C2 counteraction.

7. 防守方 AI 阶段与结算7. Defender AI Phase & Resolution

防守 AI 会继续补充扫描、加固和防御。随后结算未解决的特殊攻击并检查胜负。The defender AI continues scanning, hardening, and response work. Unresolved special attacks are then resolved, followed by victory checks.

Normal attacks start around 30% and gain a small bonus against lurking targets.

特殊机制补充Additional Mechanics

蜜罐与 keyHoneypots & Key

攻击方若对蜜罐发起某些成功攻击，防守方会积累 key。累计到 3 点 key 之后，防守方才能发动 Counter C2。Successful attacks into honeypots can generate key clues for the defender. Reaching 3 key is required before Counter C2 can be used.

C2 静默C2 Silence

C2 被静默后，攻击方将无法进行激活、休眠、扫描、远程传播和特殊攻击，直到静默结束或完成 C2 迁移。While C2 is silenced, the attacker cannot activate, sleep, scan, long-range spread, or use special attacks until the silence expires or C2 is moved.

特殊攻击结算Special Attack Resolution

僵尸军团与精准打击不会立刻生效，而是在防守方阶段结束时结算。参与特殊攻击的 active 节点会进入短暂冷却。Zombie Swarm and Precise Strike do not resolve immediately. They are checked at the end of the defender phase, and participating active nodes then enter cooldown.

简明策略建议Quick Strategy Tips

攻击方要平衡“隐藏感染”和“冲击 active 数量”；防守方则要在预算有限的前提下尽快识别高风险节点，并把关键资源留给清除、夺回和 Counter C2。Attackers must balance stealthy infection with active pressure, while defenders should identify high-risk nodes quickly and save resources for clear, recapture, and Counter C2 windows.
