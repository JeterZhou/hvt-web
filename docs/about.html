关于 HVTAbout HVT

这不是“黑客演示”，而是一场策略对抗Not a hacker demo — a strategic duel

Human vs Trojan 把网络安全题材转化为一场可理解、可观察、可对抗的双人博弈：一方塑造威胁，另一方守住系统，双方都必须围绕同一张拓扑图不断博弈和推演。Human vs Trojan turns cybersecurity into a readable, watchable, playable duel. One side engineers the threat. The other keeps the system alive. Both must think through the same network topology in very different ways.

Core FantasyCore Fantasy

玩家扮演什么角色What Players Become

  • 攻击者：控制木马与感染链，决定何时潜伏、何时激活、何时发动强攻。Attacker: Commands the trojan and infection chain, choosing when to hide, when to activate, and when to strike.
  • 防守者：像安全运维与事件响应人员一样，在有限预算下扫描、加固、清除和修复网络。Defender: Operates like a blue-team responder, scanning, hardening, cleansing, and restoring the network on a limited budget.
  • 共同舞台：双方共享同一张 20 节点网络图，因此所有动作都能被具体地观察和讨论。Shared stage: Both sides play on the same 20-node network, so every choice becomes visible and discussable.

设计目标Design Goals

HVT 追求的不是复杂术语堆砌，而是让玩家能够迅速理解攻防关系，并在有限信息下作出策略选择。HVT is not about dumping jargon. It is about making cyber offense and defense legible enough that players can reason, predict, and outplay each other.
